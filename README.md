## 1. Mod Introduction

### 1.1 Items

- Chicken Coop `ikun_mod:chicken_coop`: Crafted using `minecraft:iron_bars`, used to summon the Chicken Kun. It needs to be placed as a block and then trap a chicken `minecraft:chicken` to activate. After waiting for 2500 Ticks, the Chicken Kun will automatically spawn.
    - ![chicken_coop](src/main/resources/assets/ikun_mod/textures/item/chicken_coop.png)

- Center Parted Wig `ikun_mod:center_parted_wig`: Can be obtained by killing the [Chicken Kun]. When used as a helmet, it can immunize the wearer from the [Crush on You] effect.
    - ![center_parted_wig](src/main/resources/assets/ikun_mod/textures/item/center_parted_wig.png)

### 1.2 Creatures

- Chicken Kun `ikun_mod:chicken_ikun`
    - Health: 250
    - Skills:
        - Kun Jump: Melee range attack, deals 5 damage. The hit target will receive the [Crush on You] effect for 25 Ticks.
        - Kun Shake: Ranged attack, fires a Xiao Hei Zi `ikun_mod:xiao_hei_zi`. When the Xiao Hei Zi collides with a block or an entity, it will cause an explosion of level 1. The hit target will receive the [Crush on You] effect for 100 Ticks.
        - Susan Six Forms: Ultimate skill, melee range attack. The selected target will receive the floating effect for 100 Ticks. During the floating process, the target will be locked and then cause an explosion of level 2.5. After the explosion, the target will receive the [Crush on You] effect for 180 Ticks.
    - Mechanics:
        - Immune to explosion and fall damage.
        - When the Chicken Kun has no attack target, it will automatically recover health.
        - When an entity has the [Crush on You] effect, it cannot attack the Chicken Kun.
        - Drops the [Center Parted Wig] upon death.

### 1.3 Creature Effects

- [Crush on You] `ikun_mod:crush`: The selected entity cannot attack the Chicken Kun, and the player's vision will be blocked by Xiao Hei Zis.
    - ![crush](src/main/resources/assets/ikun_mod/textures/misc/crush.png)

### 1.4 Achievements

- Beat the Chicken Kun boss `ikun_mod:adventure/beat_chicken_kun`: Obtained by successfully killing the [Chicken Kun].
